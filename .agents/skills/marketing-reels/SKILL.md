# Marketing reels

## Goal

Make one viewer understand one problem, one mechanism and one next action quickly.

## Hook rules

The first 1–2 seconds must earn attention with relevance, not noise.
Prefer:
- a recognizable operational pain,
- a specific manual task,
- a contrast between old and new workflow,
- a direct question the target customer can answer instantly.

Avoid generic hooks such as "AI is changing everything" or "This tool will change your business".

## Offer framing

Describe the mechanism, not vague magic.
Examples of useful mechanisms for FlowPilot:
- AI drafts the reply,
- operator approves it,
- response returns to the client,
- context is preserved,
- repetitive handling time is reduced.

Do not claim guaranteed revenue, guaranteed response-rate lifts or unsupported savings.

## Short-form structure

For 12–20 second videos:
1. Hook.
2. Friction/pain.
3. Product mechanism on screen.
4. Outcome in plain language.
5. CTA.

## CTA

Use one CTA only. Examples:
- "Zobacz demo"
- "Napisz FLOW"
- "Sprawdź, jak to działa"
- "Umów krótkie wdrożenie"

Choose the CTA based on where the video will run and the actual funnel.

## Testing

For A/B tests change only one primary variable at once: hook, opening visual, CTA or positioning angle.
Keep the rest stable.
