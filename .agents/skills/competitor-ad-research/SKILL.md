# Competitor Ad Research

Use this skill automatically before producing a new paid-social master creative or major campaign concept.

## Research set

Start with direct and adjacent competitors relevant to the requested job. For FlowPilot messaging/AI automation this usually includes:
- Manychat,
- Respond.io,
- Tidio / Lyro,
- Intercom / Fin,
- other current Meta/Instagram automation or customer-service AI products when relevant.

Prefer current official product pages, current campaign pages, public social creatives and Meta Ad Library evidence when accessible.

## Extract patterns, not copies

For each competitor, capture:
- first 1–2 second hook,
- pain/outcome framing,
- what product action is visually demonstrated,
- whether the creative is UGC/native, product-demo, founder-led or polished brand,
- proof style: number, logo, case study, live UI, before/after,
- CTA wording and mechanism,
- pacing and text density.

Do not reproduce another brand's wording, layout, footage, music or distinctive creative. Translate observed patterns into original FlowPilot creative.

## FlowPilot synthesis

Default synthesis for social ads:
1. Lead with an expensive or frustrating moment, not the product name.
2. Show the product mechanism within the first 3–5 seconds.
3. Prefer visible workflow proof over abstract AI claims.
4. Keep human approval/control as a differentiator when relevant.
5. Use one concrete CTA, often a keyword DM/comment CTA for Meta social.
6. Avoid fake revenue, conversion or time-saving numbers unless verified.
7. Build one master creative, then test hook/CTA variants one variable at a time.

## Output

Before production, write a compact internal creative brief containing:
- 3 competitor patterns worth borrowing at the pattern level,
- 2 patterns to avoid,
- FlowPilot's angle,
- the chosen hook,
- CTA,
- proof mechanism shown on screen.
