# Natural Polish copy

Write like a competent Polish founder/operator speaking to another business owner.

## Remove AI slop

Delete filler introductions, inflated adjectives, forced enthusiasm, vague business jargon and repetitive summaries.
Avoid translated-English phrasing when a normal Polish sentence is shorter.
Do not overuse em dashes, rhetorical triplets, "rewolucyjny", "game changer", "w dzisiejszym dynamicznym świecie", "wykorzystaj moc AI" or similar stock language.

## Prefer

- concrete verbs,
- short sentences,
- normal contractions/colloquial rhythm where appropriate,
- specific workflow language,
- one clear claim at a time.

Example:
Bad: "Zrewolucjonizuj swoją komunikację z klientami dzięki potędze sztucznej inteligencji."
Better: "AI przygotowuje odpowiedź. Ty ją zatwierdzasz i leci do klienta."

## Reels

Spoken/onscreen Polish should be even tighter than website copy. If a sentence cannot be read comfortably on a phone within the available scene time, shorten it.

## Accuracy

Do not make the copy more persuasive by inventing facts, customer counts, savings, revenue lifts or testimonials.

## Upstream inspiration

`hardikpandya/stop-slop` is a useful external anti-AI-writing reference. Adapt its ideas to Polish rather than copying English-specific patterns mechanically.
